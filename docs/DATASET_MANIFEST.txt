## Important Notes

- The target is_overdue exactly matches payment_status == Overdue.
- Do not use payment_status or payment_date as invoice-time prediction features.
- No positive payment delays were observed among paid invoices.
- Multiple currencies are present. Monetary values must not be compared directly across currencies without conversion or currency-aware analysis.

Large datasets are excluded from Git tracking. Obtain them separately to reproduce the project.
